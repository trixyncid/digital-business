Definition

What is Digital Marketing?

"Digital marketing is the use of digital channels, platforms, and technologies to promote products, services, and brands to reach and engage customers."

1

Channel-agnostic — SEO, social media, email, mobile, and more work together

2

Data-driven — Every action can be measured, tracked, and optimized

3

Two-way communication — Customers interact, not just receive

Customer Journey

The Marketing Funnel

Each stage requires different tactics, channels, and metrics to measure success.

👁️

Awareness

"I know this brand exists"
Channels

SEO, social media, PR, influencer marketing

Metrics

Reach, impressions, website traffic

📊 40-50% Budget
💡

Interest

"I want to learn more"
Channels

Blog content, email capture, retargeting

Metrics

Time on site, email signups, downloads

📊 20-30% Budget
⚖️

Consideration

"I'm comparing options"
Channels

Product pages, reviews, comparisons

Metrics

Add to cart, product views, wishlists

📊 10-15% Budget
🛒

Conversion

"I'm buying now"
Channels

Checkout optimization, promo codes

Metrics

Conversion rate, average order value

📊 10-15% Budget
❤️

Loyalty

"I'll buy again and tell friends"
Channels

Email follow-up, loyalty program

Metrics

Repeat purchase rate, NPS, referrals

📊 5-10% Budget

20 Minutes
