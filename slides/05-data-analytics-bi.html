Foundation

Why Data Analytics Matters

Companies that leverage data outperform their peers by 5-6% in profitability. Data turns guesswork into strategy.

  • Descriptive Analytics: What happened? (Reports, dashboards)
  • Diagnostic Analytics: Why did it happen? (Root cause analysis)
  • Predictive Analytics: What will happen? (Forecasting, ML models)
  • Prescriptive Analytics: What should we do? (Recommendation engines)

Key Insight: Most businesses start with descriptive and progressively move toward prescriptive as they mature.

KPIs

Essential Business KPIs

Key Performance Indicators every digital business must track.

# Revenue & Financial KPIs
MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue):
  Total monthly revenue from subscriptions
  Target: Grow 10-15% month-over-month

CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):
  Total marketing spend / Number of new customers
  Target: CAC < CLV/3 (3x return minimum)

CLV (Customer Lifetime Value):
  Average revenue per customer over their lifetime
  Target: 3-5x your CAC

# Growth & Engagement KPIs
Conversion Rate:
  (Conversions / Total Visitors) x 100%
  Industry avg: 2-5% (e-commerce), 5-10% (SaaS)

Churn Rate:
  (Customers Lost / Total Customers) x 100%
  Target: < 5% monthly for subscription businesses

NPS (Net Promoter Score):
  "How likely are you to recommend us?" (0-10)
  Score: Promoters (9-10) - Detractors (0-6)
  Target: > 50 (excellent)
Tools

Analytics Tools for Business

# Free Tools (Start Here)

Google Analytics 4 (GA4):
  Website traffic, user behavior, conversions
  Events-based tracking (replaces session-based)
  Integration with Google Ads
  Free for all businesses

Google Looker Studio (Data Studio):
  Beautiful dashboards from multiple data sources
  Connect to GA4, Sheets, BigQuery, MySQL
  Share dashboards with team via link

Meta Business Suite:
  Instagram + Facebook analytics combined
  Audience insights, content performance
  Ad campaign management

# Paid Tools (When You Scale)

Microsoft Power BI:
  Enterprise dashboards, advanced visualizations
  Integrates with Excel, SQL, cloud databases
  From US$ 10/user/month

Mixpanel / Amplitude:
  Product analytics, user journey tracking
  A/B testing, cohort analysis
  Free tier available
