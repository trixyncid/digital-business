Digital Payments
Landscape

Payment Ecosystem in Indonesia

  • E-Wallets: GoPay, OVO, Dana, ShopeePay — 70%+ of digital transactions
  • QRIS: Unified QR standard — one QR code for all e-wallets
  • Virtual Account: Bank transfer with instant confirmation
  • Credit/Debit Cards: Visa, Mastercard, JCB — growing but still minority
  • BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later): Kredivo, Akulaku, Home Credit — 30%+ growth YoY
  • COD: Cash on delivery — still 20-30% of e-commerce transactions
Integration

Payment Gateway Integration

How to accept digital payments in your business.

# Payment Gateway Options

Midtrans (GoTo):
  Supports: Cards, VA, e-wallets, QRIS, BNPL
  Fees: 2-3% per transaction
  Integration: REST API, plugins for WooCommerce/Shopify
  Best for: E-commerce, SaaS, subscriptions

Xendit:
  Supports: Cards, VA, e-wallets, QRIS, invoices
  Fees: 1.5-3% per transaction
  Integration: REST API, React/Vue SDKs
  Best for: Tech startups, marketplaces

Duitku:
  Supports: Cards, VA, e-wallets, QRIS, convenience stores
  Fees: 2-3% per transaction
  Integration: API, WooCommerce, Magento plugins
  Best for: SMEs, online stores

Moota:
  Focus: Bank transfer reconciliation
  Auto-matches payments to orders
  Best for: Businesses with high transaction volume
Recap
