Evolution

From Ledger Book to Cloud

The accounting rules stayed the same. What changed is who does the posting, where the data lives, and how fast reports appear.

Manual

Ledger Books

Every journal entry written by hand, then copied to the ledger and summed for reports.

Slow, error-prone, one copy only
1980s

Spreadsheets

Formulas do the math, but double entry and controls still depend on the person.

Excel is still common in UMKM
1990s

Desktop Software

Installed on one computer. Enter a transaction once and the journal, ledger and reports update.

e.g. Accurate (first release 1999)
2006 →

Cloud Software

Runs in the browser, paid by subscription, accessed anywhere, connected to banks and other apps.

e.g. Xero (2006), Jurnal, Accurate Online
Source: Xero company profile (xero.com/about); Accurate history in COSTING Journal 7(4), 2024.
Core Concept

What Accounting Software Does

Each module matches a business cycle you already study. A transaction entered in one module posts to the general ledger automatically.

General Ledger

Chart of accounts, journals and the trial balance.

Sales & Receivables

Quotes, invoices, customer payments and aging.

Purchases & Payables

Purchase orders, supplier bills and payments.

Cash & Bank

Bank accounts, transfers and bank reconciliation.

Inventory

Stock quantities, costing method and stock alerts.

Fixed Assets

Asset register and automatic depreciation.

Tax

PPN, withholding tax and e-Faktur data.

Financial Reports

Balance sheet, profit & loss, cash flow, instantly.

Source: Romney, Steinbart, Summers & Wood, Accounting Information Systems, 15th ed., Pearson; feature pages of jurnal.id and accurate.id.
Enter once, post everywhere
Why It Matters

Manual vs Software

Software does not replace accounting knowledge. It removes repetitive work so you can focus on checking and analysing.

Manual / spreadsheet

  • Same data typed several times
  • Debits and credits can be unbalanced
  • Reports take days at month-end
  • Hard to see who changed what

Accounting software

  • One entry updates every report
  • Unbalanced journals are rejected
  • Reports available any time
  • Audit trail and user access rights

Evidence: a 2024 case study at PT Jordan Beauty Products found Accurate Online cut financial reporting time by up to 40% after moving from Excel.

Source: Nilvia & Wijayanti (2024), Fair Value: Jurnal Ilmiah Akuntansi dan Keuangan 7(2).

Tax Connection

From Accounting Software to Coretax

Since January 2025, the tax office (DJP) runs one integrated system, Coretax, which replaced separate apps such as DJP Online, e-Faktur and e-Bupot.

1

Accounting Software

Sales and purchase invoices are recorded, including PPN and withholding tax.

Jurnal, Accurate, Zahir, Xero
2

PJAP (optional bridge)

An official DJP partner sends invoice data host-to-host, so tax invoices are created without retyping.

e.g. Mekari Klikpajak, OnlinePajak
3

Coretax DJP

Tax invoices, withholding slips and monthly or annual returns are stored and reported here.

coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id
Three official channels for e-Faktur (DJP, Feb 2025): the Coretax portal, e-Faktur Client Desktop (only for PKP registered before 2025), and host-to-host through a PJAP. Tax rules change often, so always check pajak.go.id.
Source: DJP, Pengumuman KT-06/2025 tentang Penerbitan Faktur Pajak (pajak.go.id); pajak.go.id news on Coretax; klikpajak.id.

Decision Guide

How to Choose Accounting Software

There is no single best tool. The right one matches the business. Ask these six questions before signing up.

Business size & type

Trading, service or manufacturing? How many transactions per month?

Do we need inventory or production costing?

Indonesian tax support

PPN, PPh withholding and e-Faktur or PJAP connection.

Can it produce the data Coretax needs?

Integrations

Banks, marketplaces, POS, payroll and tax apps.

Can our sales channels feed it automatically?

Users & access control

Separate roles for cashier, purchasing, accountant and owner.

Can we limit who edits or approves?

Total cost

Licence or subscription, training, setup and add-ons.

What will it cost per year, not per month?

Support & trial

Local-language help, tutorials and a free trial period.

Can we test it with our own data first?
