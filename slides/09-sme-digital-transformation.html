SME Digital
Transformation

How small and medium enterprises can leverage technology to compete in the digital economy.

SME Digital Journey
Opportunity

Why SMEs Must Go Digital

  • Wider market reach — no longer limited by physical location
  • Lower operational costs compared to traditional methods
  • Access to data and customer insights
  • Fair competition with larger brands
  • Government support programs available

The Challenge: 99% of Indonesian businesses are SMEs, but only 13% have adopted digital tools. This is both a challenge and an enormous opportunity.

Roadmap

4-Step Go-Digital Roadmap for SMEs

A practical, phased approach that any SME can follow.

# Phase 1: ONLINE PRESENCE (Month 1-2)
  Cost: Free - Rp 500,000

  [DONE] Google My Business profile (free)
  [DONE] Instagram + WhatsApp Business accounts (free)
  [DONE] List on 1-2 marketplaces (Shopee/Tokopedia)
  [DONE] Basic product catalog with photos

# Phase 2: DIGITAL PAYMENT (Month 2-3)
  Cost: Free (QRIS is free from banks)

  [DONE] Activate QRIS from your bank
  [DONE] Accept GoPay/OVO/Dana
  [DONE] Set up simple POS system (Moka/Pawoon)
  [DONE] Create WhatsApp Business catalog

# Phase 3: DIGITAL MARKETING (Month 3-6)
  Cost: Rp 500,000 - 2,000,000/month

  [DONE] Content calendar (3-4 posts/week)
  [DONE] Small paid ads (Rp 10,000-50,000/day)
  [DONE] WhatsApp broadcast for promotions
  [DONE] Google My Business posts (local SEO)

# Phase 4: DIGITAL OPERATIONS (Month 6+)
  Cost: Varies by solution

  [DONE] Inventory management software
  [DONE] Accounting software (Jurnal, Accurate)
  [DONE] Customer data collection & CRM
  [DONE] Analytics dashboard (basic reporting)
Case Study

Success Stories: Indonesian SMEs

# Real Examples of SME Digital Transformation

Erigo (Fashion):
  Started: Bandung, manual production
  Digital: Instagram marketing, e-commerce, TikTok Shop
  Result: Rp 300B+ annual revenue, international expansion
  Key: Strong visual branding on Instagram

Kopi Kenangan:
  Started: Single kiosk in Jakarta
  Digital: App-based ordering, loyalty program, data analytics
  Result: 900+ stores, unicorn valuation (US$ 1B+)
  Key: Mobile-first ordering experience

Mitra Bukalapak:
  Started: Warung digitalization program
  Digital: WhatsApp ordering, digital inventory, QR payments
  Result: 30+ million Mitra partners across Indonesia
  Key: Making technology accessible for traditional sellers

Maxim (Transport):
  Started: Budget taxi alternative
  Digital: App-based, lower commission model
  Result: Competing with Gojek/Grab in price-sensitive segments
  Key: Understanding price-conscious market segment
Recap

Key Takeaways

  • 01

    Start Small, Think Big

    Google My Business + Instagram is enough to start. Don't overcomplicate.

  • 02

    QRIS is Mandatory

    Free, universal, and expected by customers. No excuse not to have it.

  • 03

    Consistency > Perfection

    Regular posting beats perfect content posted rarely. Just start.
