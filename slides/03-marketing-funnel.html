Customer Journey

The Marketing Funnel

☕ Case Study: Kopi Nusantara
👁️

Awareness

"I know this brand exists"

Goal

Make potential customers aware of your brand and products

Channels

Social media ads, SEO, influencer marketing, PR

Kopi Nusantara — Awareness

•

Instagram Reels showing barista making latte art

•

Food blogger collaboration: "Best coffee in Medan"

•

Google Ads: "coffee shop near me" targeting 5km radius

🤔

Consideration

"I'm comparing options"

Goal

Show why your brand is better than competitors

Channels

Website, reviews, comparison content, retargeting

Kopi Nusantara — Consideration

•

Website: "Our beans are single-origin from Gayo"

•

Google Reviews: 4.8 stars, 200+ reviews

•

Retargeting ads to website visitors

🛒

Purchase

"I'm buying now"

Goal

Convert interest into actual sales

Channels

E-commerce, checkout optimization, promo codes

Kopi Nusantara — Purchase

•

"First visit: Buy 1 Get 1 Free" — drives trial

•

WhatsApp ordering for pickup (no wait time)

•

QR code table ordering for dine-in

🔄

Retention

"I'll come back again"

Goal

Keep customers coming back repeatedly

Channels

Email, loyalty programs, personalized offers

Kopi Nusantara — Retention

•

Loyalty card: "Buy 9, Get 1 Free" stamp system

•

WhatsApp group: "Exclusive deals for members"

•

Birthday freebie: "Free drink on your birthday"

📣

Advocacy

"I'll tell my friends"

Goal

Turn customers into brand ambassadors

Channels

Referral programs, UGC, testimonials

Kopi Nusantara — Advocacy

•

Referral: "Invite friend, both get 20% off"

•

User-generated content: #KopiNusantara challenge

•

Google review incentive: "Free cookie for review"

💡 Key Insight: Each stage requires different messaging, channels, and KPIs. Budget allocation shifts as customers move through the funnel.
