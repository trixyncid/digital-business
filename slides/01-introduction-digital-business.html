The Digital Era
Foundation

What is Digital Business?

Digital business is any business that leverages digital technologies to create, deliver, and capture value. It goes beyond just having a website — it's about fundamentally rethinking how business works.

  • Uses internet and digital platforms as core infrastructure
  • Data-driven decision making at every level
  • Automated processes and integrated systems
  • Global reach without geographical constraints
  • Customer-centric by design, not by accident
Business Transformation
Comparison

Traditional vs Digital Business

  • Marketing: Brochures, billboards vs Targeted ads, content marketing
  • Sales: Physical storefront vs E-commerce, social commerce
  • Payment: Cash, bank transfer vs E-wallets, QRIS, BNPL
  • Data: Ledgers, manual reports vs Real-time analytics dashboards
  • Customer: Walk-in traffic vs Online traffic, SEO, paid ads
  • Scale: Limited by physical capacity vs Infinite digital scale
