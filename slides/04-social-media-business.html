Community

Do / Don’t

Do

  • Reply in business hours.
  • Thank tags; ask to repost.
  • Saved replies for FAQ.

Don’t

  • Ignore criticism.
  • Buy fake followers.
  • Spam WA broadcasts.
Paid

Ads: Small Tests

  • Meta: one objective; radius around Medan; use winning post.
  • TikTok boost: only if organic comments are positive.
  • Stop rule: pause if cost per message beats your margin.
