Big idea

Social Media Is a Sales Channel

  • Not a hobby — part of digital marketing (SEO, email, ads).
  • Comments & DMs = free customer research.
  • Local habit: see on IG/TikTok, order on WhatsApp, pay QRIS.
  • Consistency beats chasing one viral post.
