Concepts

Social Media Is a Business Channel — Not Just Posting

Social media marketing uses platforms to build visibility, trust, and conversations that support sales — online and offline.
  • Builds on Session 03: Social is one channel in the digital marketing mix (alongside SEO, email, ads).
  • Two-way: Comments, DMs, and shares are feedback — not distractions.
  • Local reality (Medan & Sumatera Utara): Many customers discover on Instagram/TikTok, then order via WhatsApp or visit a physical shop.
  • Owned + rented attention: Your profile is yours; reach depends on algorithms and optional ad spend.

Indonesia context

Platform Landscape — Where Should You Show Up?

No single “best” platform. Start where your customers already spend time and where your product can be shown clearly.

Instagram

Visual brands, food, fashion, services

Strengths: Feeds, Reels, Stories, collab posts

Example: Medan bakery shows daily fresh bread + pre-order DM

TikTok

Younger audiences, discovery

Strengths: Short video, trends, shop tab (where available)

Example: Snack brand films packing orders for Ramadan hampers

Facebook

Local communities, older demographics

Strengths: Groups, Marketplace, events

Example: Furniture shop posts in neighborhood groups

WhatsApp Business

Orders, support, catalogs

Strengths: Catalog, labels, quick replies, broadcast lists

Example: Tailor sends size chart + payment via QRIS

YouTube

Education, long trust-building

Strengths: Tutorials, reviews, Shorts

Example: Auto workshop explains maintenance tips

LinkedIn

B2B, professional services

Strengths: Articles, company page, hiring

Example: Logistics firm shares case studies

Threads

Text updates, conversation

Strengths: Quick takes, links to longer content

Example: Café threads daily menu specials

X (Twitter)

News, public conversation

Strengths: Real-time updates, customer service

Example: Brand responds to delivery complaints publicly

How to start

Building a Credible Business Profile

Before boosting posts, fix the basics. Customers decide in seconds whether you look trustworthy.

  • Name & category: Real business name; category matches what you sell.
  • Bio formula: What you offer + who it’s for + location + CTA (“Order via WA” / “Link menu”).
  • Visual system: Logo or consistent colors; templates for price posts and promos.
  • Contact & trust: WA number, hours, address or service area; highlight reviews or press if any.
  • Highlights / pinned posts: Menu, how to order, bestsellers, FAQ.
Content strategy

Content Pillars & the Value-First Mix

Pillars are recurring themes so you never stare at a blank screen. The 80/20 guideline (popularized by many social trainers) means mostly value, lightly promotional — adjust to your audience.

Educate

How-to, tips, myths vs facts — positions you as helpful expert.

Show proof

Process, ingredients, testimonials, before/after (honest, not exaggerated).

Entertain / relate

Behind the scenes, team, local culture — humanizes the brand.

Promote

Offers, new SKUs, events — clear CTA and dates.

~80% value (educate, proof, entertain)
~20% promo

Indonesia workflow

From Like to Payment: WhatsApp Business & QRIS

Social creates interest; frictionless ordering and payment close the sale. Many Sumatera Utara SMEs run discovery on IG/TikTok and fulfillment on WA.

1

Discover

Reel or post shows product; bio link / “DM ORDER” CTA.

2

Consult

WA quick replies: price list, shipping areas (Medan, Belawan, etc.).

3

Pay

QRIS on invoice image or transfer; confirm before packing.

4

Retain

Thank-you message + ask for review / next order reminder (opt-in).

Use WhatsApp Business labels (new order, paid, shipped) and saved catalog items — looks professional without a full e-commerce site.

Operations

Community Management & Customer Care

Response time and tone are part of your brand. Set realistic SLAs (e.g., reply within business hours) and document FAQs.

Do

  • Thank people for tags and reviews; ask permission before reposting faces.
  • Move complex issues to DM/WA with a ticket note.
  • Admit mistakes publicly when needed, then explain the fix.
  • Use saved replies for shipping, hours, and allergen info.

Avoid

  • Deleting honest criticism without responding.
  • Arguing in comments — take it private.
  • Posting personal political views on a business page (unless core to brand).
  • Buying fake followers — hurts insights and credibility.
Paid reach

Paid Social — When & How (Without Getting Lost)

Paid ads amplify what already works organically. Test small, learn, then scale — always label promos clearly under Indonesian advertising norms.

Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)

Good for local targeting (city, interests), retargeting website/IG engagers, and catalog sales.

  • Start with one objective (e.g., messages or traffic).
  • Use existing best post as creative — native look wins.
  • Define audience: location radius around Medan, age, interests.

TikTok / other boosts

Strong for short video discovery; monitor comment sentiment closely.

  • Boost posts that already have organic traction.
  • Track cost per message or click — not just likes.
  • Pause ads that don’t beat your baseline within a set test budget.
Discussion · 20 min

Mini Case: “Warung Kopi Menteng” Wants More Students

A small coffee shop near a university in Medan has an Instagram account with irregular posts (mostly product photos). Owner has limited time, one staff member, QRIS ready, and WhatsApp for orders. Competitors post daily Reels. Monthly sales are flat.

Your task in groups of 3–4: Propose a realistic 2-week plan — platforms, 3 content pillars, posting frequency, and how WA + QRIS fit the journey.

Which one metric would you watch first (reach, saves, DM starts, repeat orders)? Why?
What would you stop doing to free time for sustainable content?
How do you keep promo under ~20% without hiding new products?
What could go wrong publicly — and how would you respond?
Recap

Key Takeaways

  • 01

    Strategy before volume

    Pick platforms and goals; don’t spread thin across every app.

  • 02

    Consistency beats virality

    Pillars + calendar reduce burnout; improve from weekly reviews.

  • 03

    Social → WA → QRIS

    Discovery and payment should feel like one smooth journey.

  • 04

    Measure what matters

    Engagement is useless if it doesn’t support inquiries, orders, or retention.
