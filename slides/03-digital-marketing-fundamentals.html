Digital Marketing Strategy
Foundation

What is Digital Marketing?

Any marketing effort that uses digital channels to reach potential customers. It's measurable, targeted, and scalable.

  • SEO (Search Engine Optimization): Rank higher on Google organically
  • SEM (Search Engine Marketing): Paid ads on Google (Google Ads)
  • Content Marketing: Blog posts, videos, podcasts that provide value
  • Email Marketing: Direct communication with subscribers
  • Social Media Marketing: Organic and paid on social platforms
  • Affiliate Marketing: Partners promote your product for commission
Funnel

The Marketing Funnel

Every potential customer goes through these stages before purchasing.

# Marketing Funnel (AARRR Framework)

    AWARENESS (Top of Funnel)
    "I know this brand exists"
    Channels: SEO, social media, PR, influencer marketing
    Metrics: Reach, impressions, website traffic
    Budget allocation: 40-50%

    INTEREST (Middle of Funnel)
    "I want to learn more about them"
    Channels: Blog content, email capture, retargeting
    Metrics: Time on site, email signups, content downloads
    Budget allocation: 20-30%

    CONSIDERATION (Middle-Lower)
    "I'm comparing options"
    Channels: Product pages, reviews, comparison content
    Metrics: Add to cart, product page views, wishlists
    Budget allocation: 10-15%

    CONVERSION (Bottom of Funnel)
    "I'm buying now"
    Channels: Checkout optimization, promo codes, urgency
    Metrics: Conversion rate, average order value
    Budget allocation: 10-15%

    LOYALTY (Post-Purchase)
    "I'll buy again and tell friends"
    Channels: Email follow-up, loyalty program, referral
    Metrics: Repeat purchase rate, NPS, referral rate
    Budget allocation: 5-10%
SEO

SEO Fundamentals

Search Engine Optimization — getting free, organic traffic from Google.

# SEO Three Pillars

1. ON-PAGE SEO
   - Keyword research (what people search for)
   - Title tags and meta descriptions
   - Header structure (H1, H2, H3)
   - Internal linking between pages
   - Image alt text and file names
   - Content quality and freshness

2. OFF-PAGE SEO
   - Backlinks from other websites
   - Social signals (shares, mentions)
   - Google Business Profile (for local SEO)
   - Online reviews and reputation

3. TECHNICAL SEO
   - Website speed (Core Web Vitals)
   - Mobile-friendliness
   - SSL certificate (HTTPS)
   - XML sitemap and robots.txt
   - Structured data (schema markup)

# Tools: Google Search Console (free), Ahrefs, SEMrush
Exercise

Build a Digital Marketing Plan

# Individual Exercise (20 minutes)

Scenario:
  You are opening a specialty coffee shop in Medan.
  Budget: Rp 5,000,000/month for digital marketing.

Task:
  Create a digital marketing plan using the funnel:

  1. AWARENESS:
     Which channels? What content? Budget split?

  2. INTEREST:
     How will you capture leads? Email? WhatsApp?

  3. CONVERSION:
     What offers will drive first purchase?
     (e.g., "Buy 1 Get 1 Free first visit")

  4. LOYALTY:
     How will you bring customers back?
     (e.g., loyalty card, WhatsApp group)

Deliverable:
  One-page marketing plan with budget allocation
  Present in 3 minutes
