Definition

What is Digital Marketing?

"Digital marketing encompasses all marketing efforts that use electronic devices or the internet to connect with current and prospective customers."

Multi-channel approach — SEO, social media, email, and paid ads work together as an ecosystem

Measurable & data-driven — Every click, view, and conversion can be tracked and optimized

Two-way conversation — Customers interact with brands, not just passively receive messages

Customer Journey

The Marketing Funnel

01

Awareness

"I just discovered this brand"
The widest part of the funnel - your goal is maximum visibility to capture attention.
Kopi Nusantara: Instagram Reels showing latte art, food blogger visit, Google Ads
02

Consideration

"I'm comparing my options"
Prospects actively research and evaluate alternatives before making a decision.
Kopi Nusantara: Website with single-origin beans, 4.8★ reviews, retargeting
03

Purchase

"I'm ready to buy"
The decision moment - remove friction and provide clear incentives to convert.
Kopi Nusantara: "Buy 1 Get 1 Free", WhatsApp ordering, QR menu
04

Retention

"I keep coming back"
Retaining existing customers is 5x cheaper than acquiring new ones.
Kopi Nusantara: Stamp card, WhatsApp group deals, birthday freebie
05

Advocacy

"I tell my friends about it"
Satisfied customers become brand ambassadors - word-of-mouth is the most trusted channel.
Kopi Nusantara: Referral discount, #KopiNusantara challenge, review reward

Tools & Channels

Digital Marketing Media

Different channels serve different purposes — choose the right mix for your business goals.

SEO

Search Engine Optimization

Best for: Long-term organic growth, building authority

Kopi Nusantara ranks #1 for "coffee shop Medan"

Google Ads

Pay-Per-Click Advertising

Best for: Immediate traffic, targeted campaigns

"Coffee near me" ads targeting 5km radius

Instagram

Visual Social Media

Best for: Brand awareness, lifestyle content

Latte art Reels, behind-the-scenes Stories

WhatsApp

Direct Messaging

Best for: Customer service, retention, promotions

Order updates, loyalty deals, birthday offers

Email Marketing

Direct Communication

Best for: Nurturing leads, repeat purchases

Weekly newsletter with new menu + 10% discount

Content Marketing

Blog, Video, Articles

Best for: Education, trust building, SEO

Blog: "How to choose the right coffee beans"

Influencer Marketing

Third-party Endorsement

Best for: Quick reach, social proof

Analytics

Best for: Understanding what works, optimize ROI
