Definition

What is Digital Marketing?

"Digital marketing encompasses all marketing efforts that use electronic devices or the internet to connect with current and prospective customers."

Multi-channel approach — SEO, social media, email, and paid ads work together as an ecosystem

Measurable & data-driven — Every click, view, and conversion can be tracked and optimized

Two-way conversation — Customers interact with brands, not just passively receive messages

Deep Dive

Understanding Each Stage

The funnel narrows because fewer people progress to each next stage — your job is to minimize that drop-off.
👁️

Awareness

"I know this brand exists"

Theory

Top of funnel. Largest audience but lowest intent. Goal is visibility and reach.

Channels

SEO, social media, PR, influencer marketing, display ads

☕ Kopi Nusantara

Instagram Reels of barista making latte art went viral — 50K views in 3 days

🔍

Consideration

"I'm comparing options"

Theory

Prospects actively research. They compare prices, read reviews, evaluate features.

Channels

Website, blog content, email nurture, retargeting, comparison sites

☕ Kopi Nusantara

Website highlights single-origin Gayo beans — 4.8★ with 200+ Google reviews

🛒

Purchase

"I'm buying now"

Theory

Decision moment. Remove friction — easy checkout, clear pricing, trust signals.

Channels

E-commerce, POS, WhatsApp ordering, promo codes, urgency tactics

☕ Kopi Nusantara

"Buy 1 Get 1 Free" first visit — QR table ordering, zero wait time

🔄

Retention

"I keep coming back"

Theory

Repeat customers cost 5x less than acquiring new ones. Loyalty = profitability.

Channels

Email follow-up, loyalty programs, personalized offers, WhatsApp groups

☕ Kopi Nusantara

Stamp card "Buy 9 Get 1 Free" — 60% customers return within 2 weeks

📣

Advocacy

"I tell my friends"

Theory

Happy customers become brand ambassadors. Word-of-mouth is the most trusted channel.

Channels

Referral programs, UGC campaigns, testimonials, review incentives

☕ Kopi Nusantara

"Invite friend, both get 20% off" — 30% new customers via referral

Recap

Key Takeaways

Marketing is an Ecosystem

Channels work together, not in isolation. SEO feeds content, content feeds social, social feeds awareness.

Funnel = Customer Journey

Each stage needs different tactics. What works for awareness won't work for retention.

Measure Everything

If you can't measure it, you can't improve it. Use analytics to optimize every stage.
